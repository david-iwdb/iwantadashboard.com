Our philosophy

"Reports fail not because of bad data — but because they demand too much from the reader."

Most reports dump everything on one page and ask users to filter their way to an answer. That's backwards. We design around a simple idea: start with the overview. Zoom in on what matters. Drill through to the detail. It's how the human brain actually works — and it's how every report built on our framework is structured, by default.
