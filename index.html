How it works

Bring your schema. Build freely. Plug & play.

Upload & confirm your schema

Start with a thin .pbix connected to a semantic model. We read what we can from the file — you confirm which fields are available for reporting. No data ever leaves your environment.

Build in the sandbox

Choose your pages, views, and visuals. Iterate freely — flip layouts, try multiple versions, adjust navigation. Everything renders instantly with dummy data.

Plug & play

Download a fully compiled .pbix — structured, styled, and production-ready. No finishing work, no loose ends.

We never need your data. Just the structure of it.
